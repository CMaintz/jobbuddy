Who we are

Jobbuddy helps you find roles and write job applications. We are based in the EU and act as the data controller for the personal data described below. Questions and requests: contact us via the email address on your account page.

What we store

  • Account data — your email address and sign-in method (email/password, Google, or LinkedIn), managed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Career profile — the CV content, work history, skills, photo, and contact details you add or import.
  • Applications and documents — the jobs you save, the applications you track, and the documents generated for you (including notes, recruiter details, and outcomes you record).
  • Writing style — the style rules you write or derive from writing samples you provide.
  • Preferences — matching preferences, generation defaults, and notification settings.

AI processing

Document generation and analysis use OpenAI as a processor. Your name, email address, phone number, photo, and profile links are never included in the career profile sent to the AI provider — identity details are added to documents on our servers after generation. Text you explicitly submit for processing (pasted CVs, job descriptions, writing samples) is sent as provided, with obvious contact lines stripped from writing samples first. AI requests are not used by us to train models.

Cookies and local storage

Jobbuddy sets no tracking or advertising cookies and uses no analytics trackers. Your browser's local storage holds only what the app needs to function: your sign-in session (Firebase), theme choice, unsaved form drafts, and display preferences. This storage is strictly necessary, so no consent banner is required; you can clear it at any time via your browser.

Legal basis and retention

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and, for optional features like email digests, based on your settings (art. 6(1)(a), withdrawable anytime). Your data is kept until you delete it or your account; deleting your account permanently removes your profile, applications, documents, and preferences.

Your rights

  • Access & portability — export everything as JSON under Settings → Privacy & data.
  • Rectification — all profile data is directly editable in the app.
  • Erasure — delete your account under Settings → Privacy & data; this is immediate and irreversible.
  • Complaint — you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Denmark: Datatilsynet).

International transfers

AI processing by OpenAI may involve transfers to the United States, safeguarded under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. All other data is stored within the EU.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and adjust the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
